# Let's Connect

One of the best parts of writing Analyst in Action has been the conversations it has started.

Whether you have a question about a post, want to discuss a Power BI challenge, spotted something that could be improved, or simply want to connect with someone equally passionate about analytics, I'd love to hear from you.

## What You Can Reach Out About

- Questions about an article or newsletter edition
- Power BI, Microsoft Fabric, DAX, or enterprise BI discussions
- Suggestions for future topics you'd like to see covered
- Corrections or feedback that can make the content better
- Speaking, collaboration, or partnership opportunities

## A Small Favour

If you're sharing a technical problem, please avoid sending confidential company or customer data.

Where possible, anonymise names, remove sensitive information, or recreate the issue using sample data. It makes it much easier for us to have a productive conversation.
