# Curated Power BI Training

### Because No Two Power BI Journeys Are the Same

Everyone learns Power BI differently.

Some people are comfortable building reports but struggle with DAX. Others understand the fundamentals but lack confidence when working with larger models, debugging issues, or making design decisions.

That's why this programme is curated rather than taught from a fixed syllabus.

Every engagement begins by understanding where you are today, where you want to go, and the challenges you're facing. From there, the learning plan is built around you and not the other way around.

---

## Why This Approach?

Over the years, I've realised that most people don't struggle because Power BI is difficult. They struggle because they're trying to bridge the gap between tutorials and real projects on their own.

This programme is built around the same challenges I work with every day in enterprise Power BI environments, modelling decisions, DAX, performance, debugging, maintainability, and translating business requirements into scalable solutions.

Rather than simply teaching features, the focus is on helping you develop the confidence and judgement to solve real Power BI problems independently.

---

## What You'll Learn

Every learning plan is different, so the topics below are tailored to your experience and goals. Typical areas include:

### Core Power BI

- Data modelling and semantic models
- Power Query and data preparation
- DAX fundamentals through advanced calculations
- Understanding filter context and evaluation behaviour

### Debugging & Optimisation

- Troubleshooting incorrect calculations
- Improving report and model performance
- Debugging techniques and best practices

### Enterprise BI

- Designing scalable solutions
- Report and semantic model best practices
- Building solutions that remain maintainable as requirements evolve

## Is This Right for You?

### Individual Professionals

This programme is designed for professionals who want to move beyond simply building reports.

It may be a good fit if you:

- Want to strengthen your DAX and modelling skills
- Learn best by solving practical problems
- Want personalised guidance instead of a one-size-fits-all course
- Are looking to become more confident working on real Power BI projects

### Teams & Organisations

I also work with teams looking to strengthen their Power BI capability through practical, hands-on learning.

Sessions can be tailored around your team's goals, existing reports, and real business challenges, with topics including:

- Power BI best practices
- Semantic modelling
- DAX
- Performance optimisation
- Enterprise BI design principles
- Report reviews and coaching sessions

---

## Why It's Different

This isn't a certification bootcamp or a course designed to complete a checklist.

If you're already confident with a topic, we'll move on. If something needs more attention, we'll spend the time to understand it properly.

The objective isn't to cover every Power BI feature. It's to help you build the judgement, problem-solving skills, and technical confidence that carry over into every project you work on.

---

## How the Programme Works

A typical engagement includes:

- An initial assessment of your current skills and learning goals
- A personalised 5 week learning plan
- Five weekly live sessions (approximately two hours each)
- Practical exercises, discussions, and real-world examples
- Continuous adjustments as your confidence and understanding grow
- Remote delivery, with in-person sessions available where feasible
